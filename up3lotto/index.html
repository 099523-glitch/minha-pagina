Responsible play

Keep Lottery Play in Its Proper Place

Lottery participation should be treated as entertainment — not as an income source, an investment strategy, a solution to debt, or a retirement plan. This guide is built around that principle from the first page to the last.

Who it’s for

For players thinking beyond the next draw

  • Anyone who plays regularly and wants a defined budget for it
  • Players who’ve noticed their spending drift upward over time
  • People who want limits decided calmly, in advance
  • Anyone who wants lottery play to stay a small, contained form of entertainment

Who it’s not for

This is not financial advice and not a substitute for saving, investing, or professional guidance. If lottery play is causing harm, contact a responsible-gambling helpline in your jurisdiction.
