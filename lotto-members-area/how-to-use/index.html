How to Use

Learn how to get the most out of your account.

1. Browse lotteries

Open Lotteries or tap a state on the map to see the latest winning numbers for every game in that state.

2. Generate Smart Picks

Every game has a Smart Pick that builds combinations from hot & overdue numbers. Tap "Smart Pick Again" for a fresh set.

3. Study the stats

Use Frequency Chart, Number Matcher and Jackpot Analysis on each game to make informed picks.
